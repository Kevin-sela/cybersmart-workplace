# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## 1. Product summary
**Product:** CyberSmart Workplace  
**Course:** Cybersecurity Awareness for Working Professionals  
**Primary users:** Working professionals with mixed levels of digital confidence and variable internet access.

## 2. Problem
Professionals may encounter phishing, unsafe links, weak passwords, social engineering and insecure handling of workplace information. Traditional one-off presentations can be difficult to revisit and do not always provide practice or measurable feedback.

## 3. Product goal
Provide a short, mobile-friendly learning experience that helps learners recognise common cyber threats and choose safer actions in realistic workplace scenarios.

## 4. Objectives and success measures
- At least 80% of learners score 80% or higher on the end-of-module quiz.
- At least 85% of enrolled learners complete the sample module.
- At least 80% of survey respondents rate the module as easy to navigate.
- Learners can download core text resources for use when connectivity is limited.

These are proposed targets for evaluation, not measured results.

## 5. MVP scope
- Course landing page and module overview.
- Two content formats: concise reading material and a short scenario-based visual/video resource.
- Discussion forum for moderated peer discussion.
- Collaborative group task: produce a workplace phishing-response checklist.
- Ten-question quiz with immediate explanations for correct and incorrect answers.
- Basic progress/completion tracking.
- Learner feedback survey with 6–8 questions.
- Accessible layout, responsive screens and low-bandwidth content.

## 6. Out of scope for the first prototype
- Payments, public course marketplace, certificates with external verification.
- Collection of real workplace incident reports or confidential company data.
- Advanced analytics or integration with a production HR/LMS system.

## 7. Main user stories
- As a learner, I can view the course and see what I need to complete.
- As a learner, I can study text-based content on a phone or laptop.
- As a learner, I can answer a quiz and receive immediate feedback.
- As a learner, I can discuss a scenario with peers using respectful guidelines.
- As an instructor, I can review completion and quiz results.
- As an instructor, I can collect anonymous satisfaction and usability feedback.

## 8. Constraints and assumptions
- Course duration is planned for 4 weeks, within the assignment's 4–6 week requirement.
- Learners may have limited data, intermittent power or older devices.
- Prototype content must not contain confidential client information.
- Usernames and passwords for lecturer access must be created and tested before submission; none are invented in this planning document.

## 9. Acceptance criteria
- All required prototype elements are present and navigable.
- Quiz has at least 10 questions and meaningful feedback for every answer.
- Core content remains readable on mobile screens and at increased text size.
- A tester can complete the module without verbal assistance.
- Test findings and resulting revisions are recorded.
